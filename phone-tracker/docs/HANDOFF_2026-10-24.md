## What was decided on Oct 23

1. **A website instead of an iPhone-only app**, because Apple builds on Windows. It runs in Chrome or Edge on a PC, Chrome on Android, and the **Bluefy** browser on iPhone (Safari can't use Bluetooth). The Swift app is a backup.
2. **Steam = a humidifier whose mist follows the person.** Apple's reason: the mist stays on you when you move from bed to chair, and helps wheelchair users who can't re-aim a nozzle. The nozzle is on the head beside the phone; tank, mist maker and fan sit beside the base. One MOSFET on pin **A0 (GPIO 26)**; not pin 14.
3. **Hardware = Kris's SightLine head**: two LD2410C radars toed out ±25°, the iPhone screen-forward above them (so the website defaults to the front camera). Merge proposed in PR #7. The camera steers while the website keeps sending aims; the radars take over when they stop for 0.7 s (dark room, FaceTime call, person lost).
4. **Pan 10–170° (160°).** 360° is a pitch talking point only: a stepper or multi-turn servo, a slip ring, and mist up the hollow pan axis (`docs/DEMO_AND_PITCH.md`).

## State of the software

**Works and tested**
- 13 automated tests: lock on, crossing paths, walking out of the picture, blanket hiding (with and without radar), following a moving blanket, a simulated room with a slow motor, the wall-and-coat bug, a coat that looks very similar, lighting drift.
- The real page in Chrome on Apple's PC, with a street photo and a real street video ("Calle preciados", Madrid) fed in as the camera. It stayed on the chosen man among about 15 people, followed him off the edge, didn't grab strangers, and re-found him when he came back.
- Speed: about 8 fps on this laptop's Intel UHD graphics. Detection is about 70 ms per frame on the GPU and 380 ms on the CPU, so the GPU is used.

**The uncommitted fix (late Oct 23)**
- **Apple's report:** on the PC webcam, after going behind a wall and coming back, the green box stopped moving for good.
- **Cause:**
  - The tracker trusted closeness over looks, so it latched onto something near the wall.
  - It then rewrote its saved look with that thing's colours within a second or two.
  - The colour fingerprint scored red, orange and brown as identical.
- **Fix:**
  - The look from the moment of the tap is kept permanently. It adapts slowly, and only while sure.
  - A "sort of" match reached by a jump is trusted for at most 1.5 s. A smoothly followed box stays trusted even as colours drift.
  - Someone who clearly matches anywhere wins: under 0.30 to switch away, under 0.38 to re-find.
  - Finer colour bins (16 hues × dark/light + 5 greys, blended at the edges).
  - The match number now shows on every box.
  - Errors in the frame loop now show on screen instead of freezing.
- **Calibration** from the street video:

| Comparison | Median | 90th percentile |
|---|---|---|
| Same person, within 1 s | 0.16 | 0.31 |
| Same person, more than 1 s later | 0.28 | 0.58 |
| Strangers | 0.54 | (only 9% under 0.38, 1.4% under 0.30) |

## Testing on a phone (Apple's Samsung Android phone)

  1. The site must listen on IPv4. Plain `npm run dev` listens only on `[::1]`. Run `npx vite --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5175 --strictPort` from the repo root.

If the file is gone, ask before downloading it again from github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared/releases, and Apple must allow the new path in simplewall.

## Gotchas learned this session

- **simplewall** blocks every new program from the internet ("forbidden by its access permissions"). Give Apple the exact .exe path to allow. Never change firewall settings yourself.
- **Low memory:** Claude Code stops idle background shells when the PC is low on memory. Don't restart them unasked. Their Node child processes can survive and keep holding ports. Check with `Get-NetTCPConnection`, and only stop processes this session started.
- **Vite live reload** wipes any in-page test setup when a file changes. Finish code edits before browser tests.
- **Testing without a webcam** (and without touching Apple's camera): in the page, replace `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` with a function returning a canvas `captureStream()`. Draw a Wikimedia Commons photo onto the canvas ("File:Pedestrians using crosswalk (Unsplash).jpg"), or a video ("File:Calle preciados.ogv"; use its 480p webm derivative, because Chrome can't play Ogg). Load with `crossOrigin = "anonymous"` and API calls with `origin=*`.
- **MediaPipe** `@mediapipe/tasks-vision` is pinned at 1.1.1. `MEDIAPIPE_VERSION` in `src/tracker/detector.ts` must match, because the WASM loads from jsDelivr. The model loads from Google's storage.
- **Session names change** when Claude Code restarts. Use ListAgents. The 3D-mockup session is `projects-c5` as of writing.
